\section{Conclusion}

In this paper, we address the root inefficiency of long-context LLM inference by formalizing KV Admission as a critical third primitive alongside KV Selection and Eviction. We introduce Write-Gated KV (WG-KV), a learnable mechanism that predictively filters redundant tokens pre-write, achieving substantial efficiency gains while preserving near-lossless task accuracy. Backed by our hardware-aware implementation and extensive evaluations, we demonstrate that \textit{learning what to write} provides a principled and practical recipe for efficient long-context inference.
